# Atlas map data sources

## Landmass (orthographic globe dot lattice)

- **Source**: Natural Earth, `ne_110m_land` (110m resolution — Natural Earth's coarsest, made for exactly this kind of small-scale/whole-world display). Unchanged from before this became a globe — see Open items/history below for why a coarser source is still the right call even at the globe's much closer max zoom.
- **Fetched from**: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson` (the project's official GitHub mirror).
- **License**: Public domain. Per Natural Earth's own terms of use (naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/): *"All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public domain... No permission is needed to use Natural Earth. Crediting the authors is unnecessary."* Attribution is optional/encouraged, not required.
- **How it's used**: `scripts/generate-atlas-land-mask.mjs` reads the source polygons and rasterizes them with the shared scanline rasterizer `scripts/lib/land-raster.mjs` (spec 016; same ray-casting rule as before, per-polygon parity, respecting holes — the regenerated mask is byte-identical to the old one, sha256 `948d1af1…6ac9`, `specs/016-atlas-visor/tasks.md`, T4) into a monochrome 2048×1024 equirectangular PNG — `public/atlas/land-mask.png` (land = white, ocean = black; ~23KB, compresses well since land/ocean are large contiguous regions). That's the only shipped artifact; no raw GeoJSON and no per-dot list ship to the client.
- **Why a raster mask now, not a fixed dot list**: the old flat map baked one dot grid in world coordinates and scaled it with zoom, so on-screen spacing degraded linearly as you zoomed in (measured: 31.3px spacing at one zoom, ~6.7px at scale 1 — zooming in just showed the SAME dots further apart, never more coastline). The globe instead derives the lattice's angular step from the CURRENT radius every time it changes meaningfully (`TARGET_PX / R`, see `draw()` in `src/motion/Atlas.tsx`; since spec 016 only the visible part of the lattice is walked, `visibleRange`, `src/motion/Atlas.tsx:379`) and resamples the mask at that step — on-screen spacing stays pinned to `TARGET_PX` (7px) at every zoom level. **The mask sets the zoom ceiling** (spec 016): past R = 2048 × 14 / 2π ≈ 4563 px one mask pixel covers more than 14 screen px — two lattice dots — and the coastline shows as steps, so outside a local zone the globe stops there (`WORLD_MAX_R`, `src/motion/Atlas.tsx:64`; measured, `evidencia/2026-11-02_016-zoom.txt:71`).
- **Regenerating**: see the header comment in `scripts/generate-atlas-land-mask.mjs`. The source GeoJSON itself isn't checked in (a build input, same convention as the gitignored `photos-master/`) — only the generated PNG mask is.

## Local coastline masks (spec 016)

- **What**: where photos with `precision: "exact"` sit too close together to separate on the world mask, a ZONE gets a small pyramid of square equirectangular masks, 1024×1024 each: 400 km, then ten times smaller, until the finest resolves the zoom its closest pair needs (`scripts/generate-atlas-local-masks.mjs:42-43`). Today: San Sebastián (400 / 40 / 9.1 km, down to 8.9 m/px) and New York City (400 / 40 km) — 5 PNGs in `public/atlas/local/`, described in `src/data/atlas-zones.json` (bbox, resolution limit and needed R per level). San Miguel de Allende is excluded by Javi's decision (`EXCLUDED_CITIES`, `scripts/generate-atlas-local-masks.mjs:49`): its photos stay grouped. Zones are derived from `src/data/photos.ts`, so new photos need no component change — only a re-run of the script; the diagnostic's CA10 (`atlas` section) fails if one is forgotten (`evidencia/2026-11-02_016-pin.txt:84`).
- **Source**: OpenStreetMap land polygons, `land-polygons-split-4326` from osmdata.openstreetmap.de (OSM's coastline already assembled into land polygons, WGS84, split into 1° tiles).
- **Fetched from**: `https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/download/land-polygons-split-4326.zip` (~930 MB zip, ~1.3 GB shapefile). Kept in `atlas-source/`, gitignored — a build input, like `photos-master/`.
- **License**: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Attribution is **required**: "© OpenStreetMap contributors". It is shown on the atlas itself, under the legend, linking to openstreetmap.org/copyright (Javi's decision, 2026-11-01: on the atlas only, not on `/credits`; diagnostic CA12). The generated PNGs are a Produced Work; the ODbL's share-alike applies to derived databases, not to them.
- **How it's used**: the script reads the shapefile with its own minimal reader (the `.shx` index plus each record's leading bbox, so only the ~1800 records that touch a zone are parsed; no dependency), rasterizes with `scripts/lib/land-raster.mjs`, and runs in ~2 s. In the browser the masks are fetched lazily (`fetch` + `createImageBitmap`) the first time the view nears a zone, and the lattice samples the finest loaded mask covering each dot, cross-faded with the coarser one so nothing pops (`src/motion/Atlas.tsx:65-67`). The zoom ceiling at the centre of the view becomes that of the finest loaded level there, capped at the zone's need (`currentMaxR`, `src/motion/Atlas.tsx:769`).
- **Regenerating**: download and unzip as the header of `scripts/generate-atlas-local-masks.mjs` says, then `node scripts/generate-atlas-local-masks.mjs`. It rewrites `public/atlas/local/` and `src/data/atlas-zones.json`.
- **Known limit**: where a local mask meets the world mask the coastline changes detail (a seam). Judged by Javi when trying it (spec 016, CA9).

## Photo coordinates

Hand-entered by Javi per photo (`src/data/photos.ts`, `Place` type) — not from any external dataset, not from EXIF. See that file's doc comments and `design-spec-v2.md`'s "Place data" section for the exact rules (`exact` vs `city` precision, nullability).
